History Rewards Creators of the New World, Not Optimizers of the Old

Nokia — Optimized the Old World 2007
  • ✕ Built the most reliable mobile phones on Earth.
  • ✕ Focused on hardware durability and battery life.
  • ✕ Competed on keyboard tactile feel and signal strength.

  • ✓ Refused to compete on keyboards or physical reliability.
